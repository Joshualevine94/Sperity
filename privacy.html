Privacy policy

What is collected, why, and what happens to it. Written to be read rather than to protect anybody, so it is short.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what Sperity collects, why, and what happens to it. It is written to be read rather than to protect anybody, so it is short.

Who this is

Sperity is a personal budgeting app. Questions, complaints or requests to delete your data: Joshualevine94@gmail.com.

What is collected

What you type in. Your bills, debts, savings goals, paychecks and the balances you enter. This is the app's whole purpose.

Your email address and name, when you make an account, so you can sign in and be addressed properly.

Bank transactions and balances, if and only if you connect a bank. This comes through Plaid, and covers the accounts you choose — transaction dates, amounts, merchant names, categories and balances.

Nothing else. No advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no device fingerprinting, no analytics on how you use the app. There are no third-party trackers in it.

What is never collected

Your bank username and password. Sign-in happens on your bank's own page through Plaid. Neither this app nor its server ever sees those credentials.

Where it is kept

Your bills and balances are stored in a Supabase database, encrypted at rest, under your account. Access is enforced by the database itself: every row belongs to a user, and a request for somebody else's rows returns nothing.

Bank access tokens are held server-side only, in a table the app has no permission to read. They never reach a phone.

A copy of your data is also kept on your own device so the app works without a signal.

All traffic uses HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or better.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, for any commercial purpose. Your financial data is not sold, not rented, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone for their own purposes.

Three services process data to make the app work:

  • Plaid connects your banks and supplies transactions. Their policy: plaid.com/legal
  • Supabase stores your account and data. Their policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Anthropic categorises merchants and reads PDF statements, *only if you turn AI features on*. Merchant names and figures are sent; your name, email and account identifiers are not. Their policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Cloudflare runs the server that connects these together. It processes requests but stores nothing beyond the bank tokens described above.

Your choices

AI features are off unless you turn them on. With them off, nothing is ever sent to Anthropic.

Bank connections are optional. The app works entirely on manually entered figures and imported statements. Disconnecting a bank in the app also tells Plaid to delete the connection.

An account is optional. There is a "use it on this phone only" choice, where nothing leaves the device at all.

Deleting your data

Erase all data in Settings removes everything from your device. To delete your account and everything stored on the server, email Joshualevine94@gmail.com and it will be removed within 30 days, including telling Plaid to drop any bank connections.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. Delete the account and it goes.

Children

Not intended for anyone under 18, and no data is knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially you will be told in the app before the change takes effect.
