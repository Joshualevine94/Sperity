You run the house.
This runs the numbers.

Every bill, every payday, every account — in one place, answering the only question that matters: what can I actually spend right now?

Most money apps are a rear-view mirror — here is what you spent, try harder next month. Sperity looks forward instead. It knows what is coming out before your next paycheck, so the number on the front screen is the one that actually decides whether you can say yes to something.

What it does

Unspoken for

What is in the bank, minus every bill, card payment and savings contribution still to land before payday.

Thinks in pay periods

Not calendar months. Rent on the 30th against a paycheck on the 2nd is the problem worth solving.

Bills run themselves

Enter something once and it repeats on whatever rhythm it actually has — monthly, fortnightly, quarterly, certain weekdays.

Before you buy

Put in an amount and see what it does to this pay period, the month, and the three periods after it.

Your banks, optional

Connect accounts and transactions arrive on their own, or import a statement, or type things in. All three work.

Budgets that bite

A budget can hold its money back, so setting some aside for petrol stops it counting as spendable.

About your data

Bank sign-in happens on your bank's own page. Neither this app nor its server ever sees your bank username or password. Your financial data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone for their own purposes. There are no third-party trackers in the app, and no analytics on how you use it.

There is also a mode where nothing leaves your phone at all.
